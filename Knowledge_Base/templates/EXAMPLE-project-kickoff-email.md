# Project Kickoff Email

> This is an example showing the format. Delete it once you have real files.

## Use when

Send this after a project is approved and the kickoff time is confirmed.

## Template

**Subject:** Kickoff for [project name]

Hello [client contact],

We're ready to get [project name] moving. Here's the goal we've agreed on:

[One-sentence project goal]

Our kickoff is [date] at [time and time zone] using [meeting location]. Before we meet, please [single preparation step] by [due date].

We'll use the session to confirm the plan, assign the first actions, and surface any open questions. Your main contact is [team role or shared inbox].

Thanks,

[Team or business name]

## Check before sending

- Replace every bracketed field.
- Confirm the date, time zone, and location.
- Keep the preparation request to one clear action.
