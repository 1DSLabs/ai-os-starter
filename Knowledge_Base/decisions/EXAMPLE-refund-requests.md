# Refund Requests

> This is an example showing the format. Delete it once you have real files.

## Rule

If a client asks for a refund, pause any avoidable new work and review the agreed scope, completed work, and committed costs.

Approve a full refund when no work has begun and no unrecoverable cost has been incurred. Once work or committed costs exist, the project owner proposes a fair resolution tied to what can be verified.

## Response

- Acknowledge the request within one business day.
- State the relevant work and costs in plain language.
- Explain the proposed resolution and when it will happen.
- Record the decision and reason without storing personal or payment details.

## Exceptions

Any contract term or applicable law takes precedence. Ask the business owner before promising an outcome when the facts are unclear.
