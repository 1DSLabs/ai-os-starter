# Writing Rules

This file ships turned on. It's the writing standard 1DS Collective runs internally, adapted for any business. Your tone lives in `CLAUDE.md`; this file bans the tics that make writing sound machine-made, whatever your tone is. Edit it to fit how you write, or delete it if you'd rather not enforce a standard.

It applies to anything written for a human to read: emails, posts, proposals, pages, scripts, internal notes. Code and tool output are exempt.

## Write like a person

- Use contractions naturally (don't, can't, won't).
- Short paragraphs, 1 to 3 sentences.
- Get to the point. No warm-up sentences before the actual point.
- Make claims specific: numbers, names, dates, concrete details.
- Vary sentence length. Mix short punchy lines with longer ones.
- When unsure, say so plainly ("I think," "probably"). Hedging honestly is human.
- Shorter and accurate beats longer and padded.
- Parenthetical asides are welcome for honest reactions and quick tangents. (Like this.)

## Formatting

- No em dashes, ever. Use commas, periods, colons, semicolons, or parentheses.
- Numbers as digits.
- Bold sparingly: 1 or 2 key moments per section.
- No arrow characters as list structure. Use normal bullets or write a sentence.

## Words and phrases to never use

- Dead AI vocabulary: `delve`, `leverage`, `utilize`, `harness`, `robust`, `seamless`, `landscape`, `realm`, `unlock`, `supercharge`, `elevate`, `empower`, `streamline`, `game-changer`, `cutting-edge`, `straightforward`, `future-proof`.
- Formula openers: `In today's ...`, `Let's dive in`, `It's worth noting`, `It's important to note`, `I'd be happy to help`, `In order to`.
- Dead transitions: `Furthermore`, `Moreover`, `Additionally`, `Moving forward`, `At the end of the day`, `In other words`.
- Engagement bait: `Let that sink in`, `Read that again`, `This changes everything`, `You're not ready for this`, `Full stop`.
- Fake insider claims: anything built on `what nobody tells you` or `most people don't realize`.
- `Enter:` as a dramatic introduction, `The best part?` as a payoff setup, and `To your success` as a sign-off.

## Patterns to never use

- The negation pivot: sentences shaped like `This isn't X. This is Y.` or `Not X. Y.` or `Less X, more Y.` Delete the negation and state the positive claim directly. This is the single loudest AI tell.
- Staccato stacks: 3 or more consecutive fragments starting with the same word, like `No X. No Y. No Z.` or `Your content, your products, your audience.` Collapse them into one natural sentence.
- Authenticity stacking: repeating `real` to perform sincerity (`real strategy, real results`). Once per piece, at most.
- `If you're serious about X, ...` before an ask. State the ask directly.

## Check before sending

1. Any em dashes? Rewrite.
2. Any word or phrase from the banned lists? Rewrite.
3. Any negation pivot? Delete it and state the claim directly.
4. Any word starting 3 or more fragments in a row? Collapse into one sentence.
5. Any warm-up before the point? Cut it.

If it passes all 5, send it.
